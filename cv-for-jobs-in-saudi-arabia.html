How to Write a CV for Jobs in Saudi Arabia in 2026

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Saudi Arabia offers employment opportunities across industries such as construction, engineering, healthcare, hospitality, information technology, finance, education and business services.

When applying for jobs in the Kingdom, a professional CV can help employers understand your qualifications, experience and suitability for a position.

Whether you are already working in Saudi Arabia or applying from another country, your CV should present your professional background clearly and accurately.

This guide explains how to prepare a CV for Saudi Arabian job applications in 2026, including useful examples, formatting advice and common mistakes to avoid.

1. Choose a Professional CV Format

A simple reverse-chronological CV is suitable for many job applications. It places your latest work experience first and makes your career history easy to follow.

A professional CV for Saudi Arabia can include these sections:

  • Full name and contact information
  • Professional job title
  • Professional summary
  • Work experience
  • Education and qualifications
  • Technical and professional skills
  • Certifications and training
  • Languages
  • Additional information relevant to the vacancy

Use clear headings, consistent formatting and readable fonts. Avoid unnecessary decorative elements.

2. Add Accurate Contact Information

Your contact information should make it easy for recruiters to reach you.

Consider including:

  • Full name
  • Professional email address
  • Active telephone number with country code
  • Current city and country
  • LinkedIn profile, if relevant

If you are already in Saudi Arabia, you may include your current city, such as Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam or Madinah.

Never include passwords, identification numbers or unnecessary private information in a CV.

3. Write a Strong Professional Summary

A professional summary gives recruiters a quick overview of your experience, professional strengths and career background.

Keep it short, relevant and specific to the position you want.

Example: Experienced Professional

Experienced professional with over 10 years of experience in project coordination, technical documentation and multidisciplinary team support. Skilled in preparing reports, coordinating project activities and maintaining accurate records. Experienced in working with diverse teams and committed to delivering quality work within project deadlines.

Example: Architecture Draftsman

Senior Architectural Draftsman with 14 years of experience in architectural drafting and construction documentation, including extensive experience in Saudi Arabia. Skilled in AutoCAD and Revit Architecture, preparing architectural drawings, coordinating design information and supporting multidisciplinary project teams.

Use only experience, software knowledge and qualifications that accurately describe your background.

4. Present Your Saudi Arabia Work Experience Clearly

Work experience is particularly important when applying for professional positions. Employers may want to understand your previous companies, project types, responsibilities and career progression.

For each position, include:

  • Job title
  • Company name
  • City and country
  • Employment dates
  • Main duties and relevant achievements

If you have worked in Saudi Arabia, clearly identify those positions and projects. If you have experience in other countries, include it when relevant.

Example: Senior Architectural Draftsman

ABC Engineering Consultancy
Riyadh, Saudi Arabia | January 2022 – December 2025

  • Prepared architectural drawings using AutoCAD.
  • Developed and updated construction drawing packages.
  • Supported architectural design coordination with project teams.
  • Reviewed drawings and incorporated approved design revisions.
  • Assisted with maintaining accurate technical documentation.

Do not disclose confidential project information or claim responsibility for work you did not perform.

5. Highlight Relevant Saudi Arabia Project Experience

For industries such as construction, engineering and infrastructure, relevant project experience can strengthen your application.

Where permitted, you can mention:

  • Project type, such as residential, commercial or infrastructure.
  • Your actual job role and responsibilities.
  • Software and technical tools used.
  • Coordination with consultants, contractors or other departments.
  • Relevant project stages or deliverables.

For example, an architectural professional might mention experience with residential developments, commercial buildings, stadiums, hospitality projects or entertainment developments.

Include project names only when you are permitted to share them.

6. Include Education and Professional Qualifications

List your education and qualifications accurately. Include:

  • Degree, diploma or certificate name
  • Institution or university
  • Location
  • Year of completion, where relevant

If a position requires a specific professional qualification, make sure your CV clearly identifies the relevant credential.

Do not describe an incomplete qualification as completed or claim a professional licence that you do not hold.

7. Add Technical Skills and Certifications

Choose skills that match the job advertisement.

Depending on your profession, these might include:

  • AutoCAD and Revit Architecture
  • Microsoft Office applications
  • Project coordination and documentation
  • Technical drawing and reporting
  • Construction documentation
  • Quality control procedures
  • Communication and teamwork
  • Industry-specific software and tools

Include relevant training, certificates and professional registrations where applicable. Check that each certificate is current if the role requires a valid or active credential.

8. Mention Language Skills

Language skills can be useful when applying for positions in Saudi Arabia, particularly in workplaces with international teams or customer-facing responsibilities.

You may list languages such as:

  • English
  • Arabic
  • Hindi
  • Urdu
  • Other languages you genuinely speak

Describe your ability accurately. For example, use terms such as basic, conversational, professional working proficiency or fluent when appropriate.

9. Include Current Location and Availability When Relevant

If you are already in Saudi Arabia, stating your current city can help recruiters understand your location.

You may also include relevant availability information, such as:

  • Currently residing in Saudi Arabia.
  • Available to relocate within the Kingdom.
  • Available to join after the required notice period.
  • Available for an interview by telephone or video call.

Only include information that is accurate and useful for the particular application.

10. Understand Iqama and Work Authorization Information

For candidates already residing in Saudi Arabia, recruiters may ask about work authorization, residency status or transfer arrangements.

Follow the employer's application instructions and provide accurate information when requested.

You generally do not need to place sensitive identification details or copies of official documents directly in a publicly shared CV. Provide documents through an appropriate and trusted recruitment process when required.

Work authorization requirements can vary depending on the candidate's circumstances and the position. Confirm the applicable requirements with the employer or relevant official authorities.

11. Make Your CV ATS-Friendly

Some employers use Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) to organize applications and identify relevant qualifications.

To improve readability:

  • Use standard section headings.
  • Choose readable fonts such as Arial or Calibri.
  • Use a simple and consistent layout.
  • Include relevant keywords from the job description naturally.
  • Avoid placing essential information only in graphics or images.
  • Follow the employer's requested file format.

For additional advice, read our ATS-Friendly Resume Guide.

12. Should You Include a Photograph?

Photograph requirements can differ between employers, industries and job advertisements.

Some employers may request a photograph, while others do not require one. Follow the specific application instructions rather than assuming that every Saudi Arabian employer expects a photograph.

If a photograph is requested, use a professional and appropriate image.

13. Choose the Right CV Length

For many candidates, one or two pages are sufficient. Experienced professionals with extensive relevant experience may need additional space.

Focus on the quality and relevance of the information instead of trying to reach a particular page count.

14. Common CV Mistakes to Avoid

  • Using incorrect job titles or employment dates.
  • Providing inaccurate information about qualifications.
  • Including irrelevant personal details.
  • Using an unprofessional email address.
  • Listing skills that you cannot demonstrate.
  • Ignoring the employer's application instructions.
  • Using complicated formatting that is difficult to read.
  • Submitting a CV with spelling or grammar mistakes.
  • Sending the same untailored CV to every employer.

15. Final CV Checklist for Saudi Arabia Job Applications

  • My name and contact information are correct.
  • My current location is accurate.
  • My professional summary matches the target role.
  • My latest employment appears first.
  • My Saudi Arabia experience is clearly presented where relevant.
  • My project responsibilities are accurate.
  • My qualifications and certificates are correctly listed.
  • My skills match the vacancy.
  • My language abilities are described honestly.
  • I have followed the employer's application instructions.
  • I have checked the final CV for spelling and formatting.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best CV format for jobs in Saudi Arabia?

A clear reverse-chronological CV is suitable for many applications. It presents your latest work experience first and makes your career history easy to understand.

Should I include Saudi Arabia experience in my CV?

Yes, if you have relevant experience in Saudi Arabia, include it clearly. Mention your job title, employer, location, dates and actual responsibilities.

Should I include my iqama details in my CV?

Follow the employer's instructions. You can mention relevant work authorization information when requested, but avoid placing sensitive identification numbers or unnecessary document details in a general CV.

Is a photograph compulsory for a Saudi Arabia CV?

Not necessarily. Photograph requirements depend on the employer and position. Follow the instructions in the job advertisement.

Should I prepare my CV in English or Arabic?

Use the language requested in the job advertisement. English is commonly used for many professional applications, while some employers or positions may request Arabic.

How long should my CV be?

One or two pages are often sufficient, but experienced candidates may need more space to present relevant career information clearly.
